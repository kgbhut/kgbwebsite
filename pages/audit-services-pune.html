Types of Audit Services We Offer in Pune

Our experienced team handles all types of audits required under Indian law:

Statutory Audit

Mandatory audit under Companies Act 2013 for all companies. We provide independent opinion on financial statements.

Tax Audit (Section 44AB)

Required for businesses with turnover above ₹1 crore and professionals above ₹50 lakhs. We file Form 3CA/3CB and 3CD.

Internal Audit

Voluntary audit to evaluate internal controls, risk management, and operational efficiency for Pune businesses.

GST Audit

GST compliance review and reconciliation. We file GSTR-9C for businesses with turnover above ₹5 crores.

Stock Audit

Physical verification of inventory for banks, financial institutions, and management decision-making.

Bank Audit

Concurrent and statutory audits of bank branches across Pune as empanelled CAs.

Documents Required for Audit

To ensure a smooth audit process, you'll typically need to provide:

Financial Records

  • Trial balance, profit & loss account, and balance sheet
  • General ledger and cash book
  • Bank statements for the financial year
  • Sales and purchase registers
  • Fixed asset register with depreciation calculations
  • Inventory records and stock statements

Compliance Documents

  • GST returns (GSTR-1, 3B, 9) filed during the year
  • TDS returns and certificates
  • Income tax returns of previous year
  • ROC filings and statutory registers
  • Bank loan documents and statements
  • Major contracts and agreements

Frequently Asked Questions

Is statutory audit mandatory in India? ▼

Yes, statutory audit is mandatory for all companies registered under the Companies Act 2013, regardless of turnover. It must be conducted by a qualified Chartered Accountant every financial year.

When is tax audit required under Section 44AB? ▼

Tax audit is mandatory for businesses with turnover above ₹1 crore (or ₹10 crore if cash transactions are below 5%) and professionals with gross receipts above ₹50 lakhs. The deadline is September 30.

What is the difference between statutory audit and internal audit? ▼

Statutory audit is mandated by law and provides an external opinion on financial statements. Internal audit is voluntary, conducted internally or outsourced, and focuses on operational efficiency, risk management, and internal controls.

How long does a typical audit take? ▼

Audit duration depends on business size and complexity. Small businesses typically take 1-2 weeks, while medium-sized companies may take 3-4 weeks. We work efficiently to meet your filing deadlines.
