At a glance

Types of audit we conduct

Our team handles the audits required under Indian law, as well as voluntary reviews:

Statutory audit

Mandatory audit of companies under the Companies Act, 2013, with an independent opinion on the financial statements.

Tax audit

Audit and reporting under the Income Tax Act for businesses and professionals above the prescribed limits.

Internal audit

Review of internal controls, risk management and operational efficiency.

GST audit & reconciliation

Review of GST compliance and reconciliation of returns with books.

Stock audit

Physical verification and valuation of inventory for lenders and management.

Bank audit support

Concurrent and branch audit assignments, as applicable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is statutory audit mandatory in India?
Yes. Every company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 must have its accounts audited by a Chartered Accountant each financial year, regardless of turnover.
When is a tax audit required?
A tax audit is required when business turnover or professional receipts exceed the limits prescribed under the Income Tax Act. The limits depend on the nature of income and the proportion of cash transactions, so please contact us to check your case.
What is the difference between statutory and internal audit?
A statutory audit is required by law and provides an independent opinion on the financial statements. An internal audit is usually voluntary and focuses on controls, risk management and operational efficiency.
How long does an audit take?
It depends on the size and complexity of the business. Smaller businesses may take one to two weeks, while larger entities can take longer. We plan the work around your filing deadlines.
How are audit fees decided?
Audit fees depend on the size of the business, the type of audit and its complexity. We share a written proposal after understanding your requirements.
