FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions before you get in touch.

What services do you offer?
We offer accounting and bookkeeping, income tax filing and planning, GST registration and returns, TDS compliance, statutory, tax and internal audits, company and LLP registration, ROC compliance and business advisory.
How are your fees decided?
Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work and the size of the business. After an initial discussion we share the scope and fees in writing before starting any work.
Do you work with clients outside Pune?
Yes. Our head office is in Pune, with branch offices in Ahmedabad and Hyderabad. For clients in other cities we work online, with documents shared digitally and meetings over phone or video.
How quickly can you start?
In most cases we can begin within a few working days of agreeing the scope. If you have an urgent deadline, mention it in your message or call us so we can prioritise it.
How soon will you respond to my enquiry?
We aim to respond to every enquiry within one business day. For anything urgent, please call or WhatsApp us on +91 63555 61824.
