Who Needs GST Registration in Pune?

GST registration is mandatory under Indian tax law if you fall into any of these categories:

Businesses with high turnover

Annual turnover above ₹40 lakhs (goods) or ₹20 lakhs (services).

Inter-state suppliers

Selling goods or services from Maharashtra to other states.

E-commerce sellers

Selling on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho — GST is mandatory regardless of turnover.

Casual taxable persons

Occasional businesses, exhibitions, or seasonal sellers in Pune.

Voluntary registration

Want to claim input tax credit or appear more credible to clients.

Service exporters

Pune-based IT, consulting, and freelance professionals exporting services.

Documents Required for GST Registration

Our team helps you gather and verify all required documents to prevent rejection. Here's the typical checklist:

For Proprietorship

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card of proprietor
  • Photograph (passport-size)
  • Business address proof (electricity bill / rent agreement)
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque or statement)

For Partnership / LLP / Pvt Ltd

  • PAN of business and all partners/directors
  • Partnership deed / MOA & AOA / LLP agreement
  • Address proof of business premises
  • Board resolution authorizing GST registration
  • Digital signature (DSC) of authorized signatory

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does GST registration take in Pune? ▼

GST registration typically takes 3 to 7 working days once all documents are submitted. With K.G. Bhut & Associates, we ensure faster processing through accurate documentation.

Is GST registration mandatory for small businesses in Pune? ▼

GST registration is mandatory if your annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs (for goods) or ₹20 lakhs (for services). It is also mandatory for inter-state suppliers and e-commerce sellers regardless of turnover.

What documents are needed for GST registration? ▼

You need PAN card, Aadhaar card, business address proof, bank account details, photograph, and business registration documents (partnership deed, MOA, etc. as applicable).

How much does GST registration cost in Pune? ▼

Government GST registration is free. CA firms in Pune charge a professional fee for handling the process. Contact K.G. Bhut & Associates for our affordable, transparent pricing.

Can you handle GST filing after registration? ▼

Yes! We provide complete GST compliance services in Pune — including monthly/quarterly GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), annual returns (GSTR-9), and GST audits.
