Who Should File Income Tax Returns in Pune?

You should file your ITR if you fall into any of these categories:

Salaried Employees

If your annual income exceeds the basic exemption limit of ₹2.5 lakhs (or ₹3 lakhs under new regime).

Business Owners

Sole proprietors, partnerships, and companies operating in Pune must file ITR every year.

Freelancers & Consultants

IT professionals, designers, doctors, and consultants in Pune with professional income.

Investors

Stock market traders, mutual fund investors, and those with capital gains.

Property Owners

Earning rental income from properties in Pune or elsewhere.

NRIs

Non-residents with Indian income or property must file ITR.

Documents Required for Income Tax Filing

Our team helps you organize all required documents for accurate ITR filing. Here's the typical checklist:

For Salaried Individuals

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Form 16 from your employer
  • Form 26AS (tax credit statement)
  • Bank statements for the financial year
  • Investment proofs (LIC, PPF, ELSS, NPS) for 80C deductions
  • Home loan interest certificate (if applicable)
  • Health insurance receipts (80D)

For Business Owners & Professionals

  • PAN, Aadhaar, and business registration documents
  • Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet
  • Bank statements (business and personal)
  • GST returns filed during the year
  • TDS certificates received
  • Capital gains statements (stocks, mutual funds, property sale)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for ITR filing in India? ▼

For individuals and non-audit cases, the ITR filing deadline is July 31. For businesses requiring audit, the deadline is October 31. Late filing attracts penalties under Section 234F up to ₹5,000.

Which ITR form should I file? ▼

ITR-1 is for salaried individuals with income up to ₹50 lakhs. ITR-2 is for individuals with capital gains. ITR-3 is for business/professional income. ITR-4 is for presumptive taxation. Our CAs help select the correct form for your case.

What documents are needed for ITR filing in Pune? ▼

PAN card, Aadhaar card, Form 16 (for salaried), bank statements, investment proofs (80C, 80D), home loan certificate, and capital gains statements if applicable.

How much does ITR filing cost in Pune? ▼

ITR filing fees depend on the complexity of your return. Salaried individual returns are most affordable, while business returns with multiple income sources cost more. Contact K.G. Bhut & Associates for transparent pricing.

What happens if I miss the ITR filing deadline? ▼

You can file a belated return until December 31 with a penalty of ₹1,000–₹5,000. You may also lose the ability to carry forward losses. We help clients file belated returns and minimize penalties.
