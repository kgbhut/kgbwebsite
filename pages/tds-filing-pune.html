Frequently Asked Questions

What are the due dates for TDS return filing? ▼

TDS returns must be filed quarterly. Q1 (April-June): July 31, Q2 (July-September): October 31, Q3 (October-December): January 31, Q4 (January-March): May 31. Late filing attracts penalties of ₹200 per day under Section 234E.

Who is required to deduct TDS? ▼

Any business or individual making specified payments above threshold limits must deduct TDS. This includes companies, partnerships, professionals, and individuals/HUFs whose previous year turnover exceeded audit limits under Section 44AB.

What are the different TDS return forms? ▼

Form 24Q is for TDS on salary, Form 26Q for TDS on non-salary payments to residents, Form 27Q for TDS on payments to non-residents, and Form 27EQ for TCS returns.

What happens if TDS is not deducted or deposited? ▼

Non-deduction of TDS leads to disallowance of expense under Section 40(a)(ia). Late deposit attracts interest at 1% per month for non-deduction and 1.5% per month for late deposit, plus penalties.
