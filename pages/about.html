Who we are

Accounting that moves businesses forward

K G Bhut & Associates was founded in 2017 with a simple idea: individuals and growing businesses deserve the same quality of financial and compliance support that large companies take for granted.

From our head office in Baner, Pune, and branches in Ahmedabad and Hyderabad, we support salaried professionals, proprietors, partnerships, LLPs and private limited companies with their accounting, direct and indirect taxes, audits and corporate compliance.

We are not just number-crunchers. We aim to give you financial clarity, so that every business decision is backed by accurate information and sound advice.
