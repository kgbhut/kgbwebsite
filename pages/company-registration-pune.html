Documents Required for Company Registration

Our team helps you prepare and verify all required documents to ensure smooth registration. Here's the typical checklist:

For Directors / Partners

  • PAN card of all directors/partners
  • Aadhaar card and one additional ID proof (voter ID, passport, driving license)
  • Latest passport-size photographs
  • Recent utility bill or bank statement (address proof)
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) – we help obtain this
  • Director Identification Number (DIN) – we apply for this

For Registered Office

  • Recent electricity bill of office premises (not older than 2 months)
  • Rent agreement (if rented)
  • NOC from property owner
  • Property tax receipt or sale deed (if owned)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company registration take in Pune? ▼

Private Limited Company registration typically takes 10-15 working days. LLP registration takes 15-20 working days. With K.G. Bhut & Associates, we ensure fastest processing through accurate documentation.

Which company structure is best for startups in Pune? ▼

For most startups in Pune, Private Limited Company is recommended due to limited liability protection, easier funding from investors, and credibility with clients. LLP is good for service-based businesses, while OPC suits solo entrepreneurs.

What is the minimum capital required to register a Pvt Ltd company? ▼

There is no minimum capital requirement to register a Private Limited Company in India. You can start with any authorized capital, even ₹1 lakh, depending on your business needs.

How much does it cost to register a company in Pune? ▼

The total cost includes government fees, DSC, DIN, stamp duty, and professional fees. Pvt Ltd registration typically ranges from ₹7,000 to ₹15,000 all-inclusive. Contact us for transparent pricing.

Can I register a company at my home address in Pune? ▼

Yes, you can use your residential address as the registered office for company registration in Pune. You'll need a recent utility bill and NOC from the property owner.
