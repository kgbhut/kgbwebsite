At a glance

Business structures we register

We help you choose the right structure for your goals, funding plans and liability needs:

Private Limited Company

Suited to startups seeking funding: limited liability, a separate legal entity and easier investor onboarding.

Limited Liability Partnership

Ideal for professionals and service businesses that want flexibility with limited liability.

One Person Company

Corporate benefits for solo founders, without the need for a partner.

Partnership Firm

A traditional structure for two or more partners, with lighter compliance.

Sole Proprietorship

The simplest set-up for individual traders, freelancers and small businesses.

Section 8 Company

For non-profit organisations working in education, charity or social welfare.

Checklist

Documents required for company registration

We help you gather and verify everything before filing. Here is a typical list:

Directors / partners

  • PAN of all directors or partners
  • Aadhaar and one additional ID (passport, voter ID or driving licence)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Recent utility bill or bank statement as address proof
  • Digital Signature Certificate (we help obtain it)

Registered office

  • Recent electricity bill of the premises
  • Rent agreement, if rented
  • NOC from the property owner
  • Property tax receipt or sale deed, if owned

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does company registration take?
A Private Limited Company can typically be incorporated in 10–15 working days and an LLP in 15–20 working days, depending on document readiness and MCA processing times.
Which structure is best for a startup?
A Private Limited Company is usually preferred by startups that plan to raise funding, because of limited liability and investor familiarity. An LLP suits service businesses and an OPC suits solo founders.
Is there a minimum capital requirement for a Private Limited Company?
No. There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement. You can start with an amount that suits your business needs.
What does company registration cost?
The total cost includes government fees, stamp duty, digital signatures and our professional fee, and varies by state and authorised capital. We share a complete estimate upfront.
Can I register a company at my home address?
Yes. A residential address can be used as the registered office, with a recent utility bill and an NOC from the owner.
