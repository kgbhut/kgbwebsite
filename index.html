About the firm

A practice built on accuracy, responsiveness and trust

K G Bhut & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Baner, Pune. Since 2017 we have worked with salaried professionals, family businesses, startups and growing companies on their accounting, taxation, audit and regulatory compliance.

Led by CA Khushbu G. Bhut, our team combines technical depth with a practical, hands-on approach. We explain what the law requires, what it means for you, and what needs to be done next, without jargon.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can’t find what you are looking for? Call us on +91 63555 61824.

What services does K G Bhut & Associates offer?
We handle GST registration and returns, income tax filing, TDS compliance, statutory and tax audits, company and LLP registration, accounting and bookkeeping, ROC compliance and business advisory for individuals and businesses.
Where are you located?
Our head office is at 304 Eagle Era, Baner, Pune. We also have branch offices in Ahmedabad and Hyderabad, and we work with clients in other cities online.
Can I work with you without visiting the office?
Yes. Documents can be shared digitally and most engagements, including GST, income tax and TDS filings, are completed online with calls or video meetings when needed.
How are your fees decided?
Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. After an initial discussion we share the scope and fees in writing before any work begins, so there are no surprises.
What should I bring to the first meeting?
PAN and Aadhaar, your recent tax or GST returns if any, business registration documents and any notices you have received. If you are unsure, just call us and we will share a short checklist.
